ModularPhaseNet in three minutes

Checking whether an answer holds together,
with three integer multiplications.

ModularPhaseNet takes the ideas of its predecessor QuantumPhaseNet and rebuilds them in integer arithmetic that runs on ordinary GPUs and CPUs. Conceptual hierarchy, the direction a text is heading, and contextual contradiction become quantities you can compute exactly — with no complex numbers and no quantum computer.

Kiyotaka Kasubuchi & Kazuo Fukiya / August 11, 2026 / Theory paper — experiments still to come
SCROLL
01 // ISSUE

Finding what is plausible but does not hold together

Some LLM errors look correct at the level of individual words yet are mutually impossible as a whole. Sentence-by-sentence checking misses them; reading the answer end to end reveals the contradiction. In practice this is the hardest kind.

🪜

Broken hierarchy

The model writes "this part is a kind of machine" and "this machine is a kind of part" with equal confidence

🧭

Drifting direction

Halfway through a long answer the argument turns elsewhere, and the closing paragraph no longer answers the question

🔁

Contradiction only across hops

A→B, B→C and C→A each look correct, but the three together cannot all hold

The third one matters most. However many local entailment checks you stack up, they never show whether the loop closes. ModularPhaseNet is built to make that loop countable.

02 // IDEA

Give meaning a clock face with tick marks

01
FINITE DIAL

Replace the continuous hand with a dial of fixed ticks

The previous paper gave word meaning a continuous "phase" — a position on a wave. This one rounds that phase onto a dial with, say, 256 tick marks. Once it is on ticks, adding and subtracting phases becomes integer arithmetic with zero rounding drift.

Continuous angles accumulate error every time you add them. On a clock face, "3 o'clock plus 4 hours = 7 o'clock" is always exact — and the same input always gives the same answer.
02
NESTED ADDRESS

A coarser dial is the higher-level concept

Coarsen a 256-tick dial to 128, 64, 32 ticks and fine-grained concepts start landing in the same sector. That pattern of merging is the concept hierarchy: dog and wolf share a sector on a coarse dial and separate on a fine one.

Like a postal code. The first digits give the region, the full code gives the street. Dropping digits always widens the grouping — and here that property is mathematically guaranteed.
03
CLOSING THE LOOP

Detect contradiction by walking a loop

For each link between statements, the model predicts "how far meaning rotated here". Walk A→B→C→A: if the rotations bring you exactly back to the start, the context is consistent; if not, it is contradictory. The test is three integer multiplications.

The same idea as closure error in surveying. If walking the loop does not return you to the original stake, some measurement is wrong. You may not know which step, but you know they cannot all be right.

One detail is essential: the rotation of each link must be predicted independently. If it were computed as the difference between the two endpoints, every loop would close by construction and the test would be empty. The paper names this pitfall explicitly and makes independent prediction a design requirement.

03 // HOW

How one document is processed

1
Assign a phase
Each token gets a tick on the dial (a group element) plus a "depth". Adapters in the top two to four layers of an existing model are enough
2
Read the hierarchy
Coarsening the dial shows which concepts merge, and therefore which is superordinate
3
Measure the direction
Each link between statements gets a predicted rotation, compared against the direction the whole text should be heading
4
Walk the loop
Triangular loops are selected and the product of rotations is checked against the identity in integer arithmetic
5
Feed it back into attention
Hierarchy, direction, contradiction and evidence enter ordinary attention as real-valued bonuses and penalties. No existing kernel has to be rewritten
6
Calibrate the risk
Those four signals plus thin evidence and semantic uncertainty form a risk score, calibrated into a probability on labelled data. Above threshold: answer, re-retrieve, regenerate, or abstain
04 // LINEAGE

Where this sits among the three papers

WavePhaseNet
Decomposes meaning into frequencies — low ones carrying the global theme, high ones local expression — and formalizes why hallucination is structurally unavoidable
arXiv:2602.14419
QuantumPhaseNet
Rewrites the same idea as a complex vector bundle with a gauge connection. Continuous and expressive, but involving complex arithmetic and quantum algorithms
arXiv:2608.15820
ModularPhaseNet
Quantizes only the phase channel onto a finite dial so everything is exact in integer arithmetic. No quantum hardware, no complex matrix multiplication, no discrete logarithms
2026.08.11 / arXiv submission pending

So this is the version that is light enough to run. But the paper is explicit that discretization does not add representational power. What it buys is exactness, reproducibility and checkability.

05 // STATUS

What is actually established so far

⚠ TO BE CLEAR UP FRONT
  • This is a theory paper; no experiments have been run. No performance numbers are reported.
  • QuantumPhaseNet came with prototype validation. This one does not, and the paper says so in both the abstract and the submission comments.
  • Instead, the evaluation plan, datasets, baselines and falsification criteria are fixed and published before any experiment (pre-registration).

PROVEN — established mathematically

  • Finer ticks bring the model closer to the continuous version, with an explicit bound on the gap
  • Coarsening the dial always produces nested groupings, so the hierarchy cannot break
  • The consistency score does not change when the local coordinates are changed
  • If no loop is contradictory, a single globally consistent assignment of meaning exists
  • The attention output does not blow up (boundedness)

NOT YET — to be tested

  • Whether hierarchy recovery, contradiction detection and risk calibration beat existing methods on real data
  • Whether "small rotation = higher-level concept" actually holds
  • How many ticks balance accuracy against cost
  • Whether any gain is explained away by extra parameters or stronger retrieval
06 // USE CASES

Where it could pay off

Consistency checks on long documents

Mechanically surface places where distant sections of a specification or report contradict each other

→ catches multi-hop contradictions local checks miss

Organizing in-house terminology

Hold the relations among equipment, parts and processes as a branching from coarse ticks to fine ones

→ hierarchy consistency comes from the structure

A gate on RAG answers

Calibrate a risk that combines thin evidence with contextual contradiction; above threshold, re-retrieve or answer "unknown"

→ fits work where abstaining beats guessing

On-premise, closed-network operation

Inference reduces to integer arithmetic and table lookups — no special hardware, no external API

→ suits data that cannot leave the building

All of these are uses still to be validated, not measured results. Anyone evaluating the method should start small, in the order below.

07 // SCOPE

What it can and cannot do

CAN

  • Expose hierarchy, direction, contradiction, evidence and uncertainty as numbers inside one model
  • Return the same verdict for the same input, every time
  • Drop into an existing Transformer without rewriting its kernels
  • Provide the basis for stopping an answer when it is risky

CANNOT

  • Eliminate hallucination. Missing knowledge, retrieval failure and citation error remain separate problems
  • Make inference faster. The quadratic cost of attention is untouched
  • Represent an irregular ontology with multiple inheritance as-is
  • Reproduce a quantum advantage classically — this method uses no quantum computation at all
08 // ADOPTION

If you try it, try it in this order

1
Start with a small dial
Begin at 32 or 64 ticks, not 256. Keep training cheap while you watch the behaviour
2
Adapters in the top layers only
Freeze the pretrained backbone and train adapters alone; widen the scope once an effect is visible
3
Restrict loop checking to triangles
Checking every loop is expensive. Sample triangles and short fundamental cycles first
4
Separate the calibration data
The data used to calibrate risk must be disjoint from the final evaluation set, or the numbers mean nothing
5
Fix the controls in advance
A plain categorical code, a phase-shuffled control, several dial sizes. Decide how you will tell a real effect from an artifact before you run anything
09 // FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a quantum computer?

No. The quantum Fourier transform, phase estimation and amplitude amplification present in QuantumPhaseNet have all been removed here. It runs on ordinary GPUs and CPUs.

Why modular exponentiation? Wouldn't a plain index do?

In terms of representational power they are equivalent, and the paper says so. Modular exponentiation is used because composition is exact, projection to a subgroup is the same operation, it works as a checksum, and results are reproducible. A plain-residue baseline is always included in the experiments for comparison.

Doesn't rounding onto ticks lose information?

It does. But the amount lost has a proven upper bound that shrinks monotonically as ticks are added. Where accuracy and cost balance is exactly what RQ5 of the evaluation plan is meant to determine.

Does inference get slower?

There is an added cost, though phase computation at inference is a lookup into a precomputed table, so the implementation burden is small. What does not happen is any reduction in attention's overall complexity — speed is not a claim of this paper.

Can it be retrofitted to an existing trained model?

That is exactly the intended use. The initial implementation inserts phase adapters into the top two to four layers and trains them with the backbone frozen.

When will results appear?

Not in this paper. Because the plan, datasets, baselines and falsification criteria are fixed in advance, any future experiments can be read directly as answers to this pre-registered plan.
