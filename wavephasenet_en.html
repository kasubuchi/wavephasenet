01 // ABSTRACT

What this paper actually claims

This paper reformulates the Transformer / Attention mechanism from the perspectives of measure theory and frequency analysis, and theoretically clarifies why hallucination is structurally inevitable. The central observation is that the embedding space functions as a conditional expectation over a σ-additive family, and that its failure to be isomorphic to the semantic truth set is the fundamental cause of the breakdown in logical consistency.

On that basis it proposes WavePhaseNet, a method for explicitly constructing a Semantic Conceptual Hierarchy Structure (SCHS) inside the embedding space using the DFT. Low-frequency components correspond to global meaning and intent, high-frequency components to local syntax and expression, enabling precise semantic manipulation in a diagonalized space. A graph structure over overlapping local windows then defines a cochain complex, quantifying inconsistency between local inferences as a coboundary-based loss.

This paper presents a theoretical framework and contains no experimental validation. What it offers is the mathematical formulation, the derivations, and the metrics that should be evaluated. The claims map below marks that distinction explicitly.

02 // MEASURE THEORY

Rewriting autoregression in measure-theoretic terms

Context as a σ-additive family

An autoregressive model factors the joint distribution into conditional probabilities. The information set generated by the past tokens \(X_{<t}\) — a Borel set — forms a σ-additive family.

factorization and σ-additive family \[ P(X_1,\ldots,X_N) = \prod_{t=1}^{N} P(X_t \mid X_{<t}), \qquad \mathcal{F}_t = \sigma(x_1,\ldots,x_t) \]

An embedding vector defined on the measurable space \((\Omega,\mathcal{F}_t,P)\) is therefore a random variable over that family, and the model as a whole is a sequential approximator of the conditional expectation \(E[\phi(X_{t+1})\mid\mathcal{F}_t]\).

\(L^p\) spaces and norm structure

The embedding space is treated as a normed (possibly incomplete) space via an inner product space. The model resides in \(L^p(\Omega,\mathcal{F},P)\), whose norm is the Lebesgue integral representing the expectation of the probability distribution.

\(L^p\) norm \[ \|f\|_p = \left(\int_\Omega |f(\omega)|^p \, dP(\omega)\right)^{1/p} \]

A decisive consequence follows: the output is not a pointwise truth value but average optimality over a probability distribution. The model maximizes average plausibility, not the truth of any individual claim.

Minkowski inequality \[ \|f+g\|_p \le \|f\|_p + \|g\|_p \]

This inequality lets distinct hypotheses \(f_1, f_2\) be linearly combined as \(f = \alpha f_1 + (1-\alpha) f_2\). Because of incompleteness, however, such a combination may denote a proposition that does not exist. The next section starts here.

03 // THEORY

The structural inevitability of hallucination

Definition

The paper defines hallucination as "an output that is optimal (high-probability) over a σ-additive family, but does not belong to the real-world truth set."

definition of hallucination \[ \text{truth set}\ T \subset V^{*}, \qquad \text{model-optimized set}\ \arg\min_f E\big[\ell(f(X),Y)\big], \qquad T \neq \arg\min L^p \]

The point of this definition is that hallucination becomes a gap between two sets rather than a failure of the model. On its own objective the model behaves correctly. It is the objective that is misaligned.

Why it is unavoidable: non-convex truth vs convex representation

Truth sets are generally non-convex and not closed under linear combination, while \(L^p\) spaces are linear and convex. That structural mismatch is the essential cause.

TRUTH SET \(T\)

Non-convex, with disconnected islands; the midpoint between two true claims need not be true. It demands Čech-type consistency — truth glued together across local patches.

EMBEDDING SPACE \(L^p\)

Linear and convex; an interpolant between any two points always exists. Statistical averaging under the law of large numbers yields only "distributions that are plausible on average".

The paper therefore concludes that while LLMs suit diverse, flexible text generation, they are fundamentally unsuited to inference demanding strict logical rigor — and it names AI agents and edge computing as the domains where this becomes fatal.

⚠ The reach of this claim
  • "Inevitable" means under this formulation. It does not rule out rebuilding the embedding space with non-convex structure — which is precisely what the paper goes on to propose.
  • Also, that hallucination arises structurally says nothing about its frequency or severity. This paper addresses the former and does not quantify the latter.

05 // DFT

Sequence-direction DFT and exact reconstruction

Definition

Let the token sequence length be \(N\), the embedding dimension \(d\), and the embedding matrix \(X\in\mathbb{R}^{N\times d}\). Applying the DFT along the sequence direction gives, per embedding dimension:

sequence-direction DFT \[ \hat{X}_k = \sum_{n=0}^{N-1} X_n \, e^{-2\pi i k n / N}, \qquad k = 0,\ldots,N-1 \]
amplitude–phase decomposition \[ \hat{X}_k = A_k \odot e^{i\Phi_k}, \qquad A_k = |\hat{X}_k| \in \mathbb{R}^d, \qquad \Phi_k = \angle\hat{X}_k \in \mathbb{R}^d \]

Amplitude \(A_k\) says how strongly a band contributes; phase \(\Phi_k\) says where along the sequence it lands. Discard the phase and you discard position — the decisive difference from FNet.

Exact reconstruction and conjugate symmetry

The DFT is invertible: the inverse transform reconstructs the embedding matrix exactly. The paper walks through actual numbers on a minimal \(d=4, N=3\) example to confirm this.

conjugate symmetry \[ \hat{X}_{N-k} = \overline{\hat{X}_k} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{real-valued reconstruction is guaranteed} \]

This symmetry has a practical payoff: only half the high-frequency components must be learned. The rest follow by conjugation, effectively halving the parameters to fit.

Correspondence with the proposed model

The minimal example demonstrates that (i) fixing low frequencies as summary vectors, (ii) conditionally generating high-frequency phases and amplitudes, and (iii) reconstructing via the inverse DFT is mathematically consistent and permits perfect reconstruction. With the soundness of the DFT established, the paper proceeds to proofs using simultaneous matrix representations over low (global meaning), mid (multi-stage intermediate semantics) and high (local meaning) bands.

  • The correspondence between bands and semantic roles is an empirical hypothesis requiring validation on real data. The paper itself frames it as an assumption "supported by the numerical example and the proof of perfect reconstructability."

Multi-stage separation into low through high bands; low set \(L = \{k : |k| \leq K_\text{low}\}\)
4

Phase Φ and amplitude A produce an intent vector \(S \in \mathbb{R}^m\), preserving positional sequence information
5
HIGH-FREQ MODULE (Generation)
A conditional generator \(G\) outputs \(\hat{X}'_H \sim p(\hat{X}_H \mid S)\); normalizing flows or a VAE are options
6
INVERSE DFT
Transform \(\hat{X}_L + \hat{X}'_H\) back to recover the embedding matrix. Light to implement in PyTorch

Integration into a Transformer

This is injection, not replacement. For each layer \(l\) and head \(h\), a short window of length \(w\) slides along the token axis and the DFT is applied. The window's low-frequency reconstruction \(g^{(l)}_t\) is added to the self-attention input as a residual.

intent injected as a residual \[ \tilde{x}^{(l)}_t = x^{(l)}_t + \alpha^{(l)} g^{(l)}_t \]

Here \(\alpha^{(l)}\) is a learnable scalar (or a channel-wise scaling vector), and the corrected representation feeds Query / Key / Value.

08 // DIMENSIONALITY

The case for 24,576 → 3,000 dimensions

Zipf's law and the 1/f spectrum

Natural language follows Zipf's law and exhibits self-similar structure, so the energy spectrum of embedding amplitudes shows 1/f-type decay.

energy spectrum \[ E(f) \propto \frac{1}{f^{\alpha}}, \qquad \alpha \approx 1.1 \sim 1.4 \]

\(\alpha > 1\) is attributed to syntactic constraints (grammar is more local than semantics) and to LayerNorm and attention regularizing the high band.

⚠ These numbers are derived, not measured

  • This claim is directly measurable — analyzing the embedding spectrum of an existing model suffices. It is the most testable part of the paper.
09 // COHOMOLOGY

How to measure consistency between local inferences

The tools for extracting global intent are now in place. What remains is guaranteeing that local inferences do not contradict one another.

Spectral entropy and band selection

Where "low frequency" ends is not decided arbitrarily. An entropy functional over the spectral distribution is defined, and the band \(\Omega\) minimizing information loss is selected.

band-selection criterion \[ H(p) = -\sum_{n=1}^{T} p_n \log p_n, \qquad p^{(\Omega)}_n = \frac{E_n \mathbf{1}(n\in\Omega)}{\sum_{m\in\Omega} E_m} \] \[ \min_{\Omega} D_{KL}\big(p \,\|\, p^{(\Omega)}\big) \quad\text{or}\quad \sum_{n\in\Omega} E_n \ge \rho \sum_{n=1}^{T} E_n \]

In other words, pick the smallest band meeting an energy-preservation ratio \(\rho\). The cumulative energy analysis above is this criterion applied to GPT-4's dimensionality.

The global intent vector \(g\)

From the selected low band, an inverse-DFT reconstruction defines a global intent embedding. Assuming position-independent intent, one averages all frequency components within the band.

global intent \[ g = \frac{1}{|\Omega|}\sum_{n\in\Omega} \Re\big(\tilde{V}_{(:,n)}\big) \in \mathbb{R}^d, \qquad G = \mathrm{IDFT}\big(\tilde{V}_{(:,\Omega)}\big) \in \mathbb{R}^{d\times T} \]

When a position-dependent reconstruction is attached to each token position, \(G\) is used instead — the sequence version of global intent.

Window coverings, cochains and coboundaries

Consider a family of overlapping windows \(\mathcal{U}=\{U_i\}\) covering the token set. Each window's local inference module outputs a section \(s_i \in \mathbb{R}^r\), and \(s=(s_1,\ldots,s_N)\) forms a 0-cochain.

coboundary operator \[ \delta s = (B \otimes I_r)\, s, \qquad U_i \cap U_j \neq \emptyset \ \Longleftrightarrow\ (i,j)\in E \]

\(B\) is the oriented incidence matrix. The magnitude of \(\delta s\) is the total disagreement between windows; driving it toward zero is what "semantic gluing" means.

Hodge decomposition and harmonic projection

In the eigendecomposition \(L = U\Lambda U^{\top}\), the subspace for the zero eigenvalue, \(\ker L\), corresponds to harmonic 0-cochains that assign consistent values across all windows.

graph Hodge decomposition and harmonic projection \[ \mathbb{R}^{mr} = \mathrm{im}(\delta^{\dagger}) \oplus \ker(L\otimes I_r) \oplus \mathrm{im}(\delta) \] \[ P_{\mathrm{harm}} = U_0 U_0^{\top}, \qquad s_{\mathrm{harm}} = P_{\ker(L\otimes I_r)}\, s \]

In practice the null-space basis \(U_0\) is computed by SVD or the Lanczos method. Crucially, this projection does not eliminate genuine obstructions arising from higher cohomology. Those remain as unavoidable semantic inconsistencies to be reduced during learning — the paper says so explicitly.

Metrics to evaluate

The paper's evaluation plan names perplexity (standard, from cross-entropy), local consistency rate (the mean of \(\mathbf{1}[\mathrm{agree}(s_i,s_j)]\) over overlapping pairs), and Zipf deviation (change in Zipf\((p)\) before and after training, plus the energy-preservation ratio). For ablation it specifies comparing \(\lambda=0\) (no cohomology), \(\mu=0\) (no spectral constraint) and \(\eta=0\) (no global prior). None of these were carried out in this paper.

12 // LIMITS

Limitations

There is no experimental validation

The paper contains no evaluation, no benchmark comparison and no ablation study. It presents a mathematical formulation and a list of metrics to evaluate. The question "by how much does hallucination decrease" cannot be answered today. This is the paper's design rather than a defect — formalize the cause first, leave the demonstration of the remedy to follow-up work.

Obstructions survive the projection

As the paper states, harmonic projection does not remove genuine obstructions arising from higher cohomology. They remain as unavoidable semantic inconsistencies to be reduced during training. The framework measures and reduces contradiction; it does not zero it.

Low frequency does not guarantee correctness

The low-frequency component is what appears consistently across a document — not what is true. Misinformation repeated at length also becomes low frequency. Global intent extraction must be evaluated independently of factual verification. The successor paper treats this explicitly as a caveat.

13 // CONCLUSION

Conclusion

This paper formulates LLM hallucination not as a training deficiency or data defect but as a phenomenon arising inevitably from the model's own mathematical structure, and presents a theoretical framework for suppressing it by introducing a DFT-based SCHS into the embedding space.

The demonstration that reducing 24,576 to 3,000 dimensions is justified by cumulative energy analysis based on Zipf's law and the 1/f spectrum constitutes the core contribution. WavePhaseNet lets semantic flow be described rigorously within linear algebra, opening a path toward next-generation reasoning-oriented language models.

What remains is (i) confirming the spectral structure on real data, (ii) evaluating performance after dimensionality reduction, (iii) separating the contributions of λ, μ and η by ablation, and (iv) measuring the local consistency rate. Some of this is taken up in the successor, QuantumPhaseNet.
