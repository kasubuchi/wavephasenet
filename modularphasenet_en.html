ModularPhaseNet

Finite-Cyclic Phase Geometry for Computable Semantic Hierarchy, Direction,
and Context Consistency in Standard Transformers
👤 Kiyotaka Kasubuchi, Kazuo Fukiya 📅 August 11, 2026 📄 16 pages, 1 figure

A classical, integer-computable discretization of the continuous complex phase geometry of QuantumPhaseNet. The real-valued hidden states of a standard Transformer are kept untouched; only an auxiliary phase channel is quantized into a finite cyclic group \(G=\langle g\rangle\subset\mathbb{F}_p^{\times}\). Concept hierarchy comes from a quotient filtration, semantic direction from oriented group elements, and contextual consistency from cycle holonomy — all computed exactly in integer arithmetic. No quantum hardware, no complex matrix multiplication, no discrete-logarithm computation.

01 // ABSTRACT

What the paper claims

ModularPhaseNet discretizes the continuous complex phase geometry introduced in QuantumPhaseNet into a form that runs in classical integer arithmetic. The real-valued hidden states of a standard Transformer are retained; what is quantized is only an auxiliary phase channel. A continuous phase \(e^{i\phi}\) is represented as \(z=g^{a}\bmod p\), so phase composition becomes group multiplication and relative phase becomes group division.

This substitution turns three structures into exactly computable discrete invariants: conceptual hierarchy from a filtration of cyclic quotients, semantic direction from oriented group elements attached to ordered pairs, and contextual consistency from gauge-invariant cycle holonomy. Three components are added — a finite-phase encoder, a quotient-filtration hierarchy module, and a group-valued connection module — and their outputs enter self-attention as real-valued bias terms.

What is proved: quantization-distortion bounds, nesting of the quotient-induced partitions, gauge invariance, a discrete integrability result for flat connections, and boundedness of the attention output. What is hypothesized: that these invariants improve hierarchy recovery, discourse alignment, contradiction detection and calibrated hallucination-risk prediction. That hypothesis comes with a pre-registered evaluation plan and explicit falsification criteria. No experiments have been carried out at this stage, and no empirical result is claimed.

01

Phase becomes finite

A continuous phase is rounded onto \(q\) ticks and stored as the group element \(g^a \bmod p\). Composition, inversion and relative phase become exact integer operations that reproduce identically on the same input

02

Hierarchy is nested quotients

Quotient maps along a chain of orders \(q_0\mid q_1\mid\cdots\mid q_L\) give a monotone refinement from coarse to fine concepts. Combined with a depth variable, they define an asymmetric ancestor relation

03

Consistency is "does it return to 1"

Independently predicted edge connections are multiplied along a cycle. If the product is not the identity, the local inferences cannot be reconciled globally

02 // FINITE PHASE

Quantizing a continuous phase into a finite cyclic group

Group parameters

Take a prime \(p\), an order \(q\mid(p-1)\), and an element \(g\in\mathbb{F}_p^{\times}\) of order \(q\). That such an element exists is the classical fact that \(\mathbb{F}_p^{\times}\) is cyclic (Ireland and Rosen 1990), and the resulting group is \(G\cong C_q\cong\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}\).

group and quantization \[ G=\langle g\rangle=\{1,g,g^{2},\dots,g^{q-1}\}\subset\mathbb{F}_p^{\times} \] \[ Q_{q}(\phi)=\mathrm{round}\!\left(\frac{q\phi}{2\pi}\right)\bmod q, \qquad \eta_{q}(\phi)=g^{\,Q_{q}(\phi)}\bmod p \]

The implementation keeps the exponent label \(a\in\{0,\dots,q-1\}\) and the group element \(z=g^{a}\bmod p\) together as a pair. This is the decisive design choice: the discrete logarithm \(a=\log_g z\) never has to be solved at inference time. Composition, inversion and relative phase all close within integer multiplication and division.

THEOREM 1  Distortion of finite phase quantization

The gap between geodesic distance on the circle and the distance recovered from the group word metric is bounded by one tick width, and the rounding defect of composition takes only three values.

distortion and composition defect \[ \left| d_{S^{1}}(\phi,\psi)-\frac{2\pi}{q}\,d_{G}\!\left(\eta_{q}(\phi),\eta_{q}(\psi)\right)\right|\le\frac{2\pi}{q} \] \[ \delta_{Q}=Q_{q}(\phi+\psi)-Q_{q}(\phi)-Q_{q}(\psi)\ (\mathrm{mod}\ q)\in\{-1,0,1\} \]

So the construction approaches the continuous version monotonically as \(q\) grows. How fast it does so is itself an object of study: it appears below as RQ5 of the evaluation plan.

Why this deserves the name "discrete phase geometry"

Endowing a finite set with the discrete topology yields an almost trivial structure. What is non-trivial here are three ingredients, and the paper explicitly limits its novelty claim to them.

NON-TRIVIAL

  • the Cayley graph generated by \(S=\{g,g^{-1}\}\)
  • the word metric on that graph (shortest number of ticks on the circle)
  • the subgroup/quotient filtration together with a group-valued connection on the token graph

DISCRETE WAVELENGTH

Since \(q\) ticks correspond to one full revolution, a smaller phase change yields a longer wavelength. The word metric changes with the generator, so the model specification fixes \(g\) and generator sensitivity is reported as an ablation.

word metric and discrete semantic wavelength \[ d_{q}(a,b)=\min\{|a-b|,\;q-|a-b|\},\qquad d_{G}(g^{a},g^{b})=d_{q}(a,b) \] \[ \lambda_{t}^{(q)}=\frac{q\,c_{s}(t)}{d_{G}(z_{t},z_{t+1})+\varepsilon_{q}} \]

As in QuantumPhaseNet, the correspondence "long wavelength = higher-level concept" is stated as an empirical hypothesis, not a theorem.

03 // HIERARCHY

A concept hierarchy from a quotient filtration

Nested quotient maps

Choose orders \(1=q_{0}\mid q_{1}\mid\cdots\mid q_{L}=q\) and set \(g_{\ell}=g^{\,q/q_{\ell}}\), so that \(G_{\ell}=\langle g_{\ell}\rangle\) is cyclic of order \(q_\ell\). The projections are modular exponentiations.

quotient projection \[ \pi_{\ell}\colon G\to G_{\ell},\qquad \pi_{\ell}(z)=z^{\,q/q_{\ell}}\bmod p, \qquad \pi_{\ell}(g^{a})=g_{\ell}^{\,a\bmod q_{\ell}} \]

THEOREM 2  Nesting of the hierarchical partition

If \(q_{\ell}\mid q_{m}\) with \(\ell\le m\), then \(\pi_{m}(x)=\pi_{m}(y)\) implies \(\pi_{\ell}(x)=\pi_{\ell}(y)\). The partitions generated by \(\pi_{0},\dots,\pi_{L}\) are therefore monotonically refined from coarse to fine concepts. The proof takes two lines. The point is that hierarchical consistency is guaranteed by the group structure itself, not by training.

Depth makes the relation asymmetric

A group phase alone can say that two concepts lie on the same branch, but not which one is superordinate. Each concept \(c\) therefore carries both a phase \(z_c\) and a depth \(\ell_c\).

address and ancestor relation \[ A(c)=\pi_{\ell_{c}}(z_{c})\in G_{\ell_{c}} \] \[ u\preceq v \iff \ell_{u}\le\ell_{v}\ \ \text{and}\ \ \pi_{\ell_{u}}(z_{v})=A(u) \]

During training each token emits a phase distribution \(\mathbf{p}_i\in\Delta^{q-1}\) and a depth distribution \(\mathbf{r}_i\in\Delta^{L}\); folding to level \(\ell\) then gives a differentiable soft ancestor score \(H(u\to v)\in[0,1]\) that coincides with the indicator above in the hard one-hot limit.

folding and soft ancestor score \[ (P_{\ell}\mathbf{p}_{i})(s)=\sum_{a\equiv s\ (\mathrm{mod}\ q_{\ell})}\mathbf{p}_{i}(a) \] \[ H(u\to v)=\sum_{\ell\le m}\mathbf{r}_{u}(\ell)\,\mathbf{r}_{v}(m)\, \bigl\langle P_{\ell}\mathbf{p}_{u},\,P_{\ell}\mathbf{p}_{v}\bigr\rangle\in[0,1] \]

Axes such as is-a, part-of, causal abstraction, function and attribute are assigned to separate heads carrying different cyclic groups and combined with weights \(w_h\). Each axis is identified through supervision, contrastive learning or sparsity regularization.

04 // CONNECTION

Semantic direction and the finite-group gauge connection

Edge connections must be predicted independently

Build a directed graph \(\mathcal{G}=(V,E)\) over tokens, sentences or propositions. Vertices carry a phase \(z_i\in G\); each edge \((j\to i)\) carries a connection \(U_{ij}\in G\) with \(U_{ji}=U_{ij}^{-1}\) representing parallel transport.

⚠ THE DESIGN FORK
  • \(U_{ij}\) must not be defined as \(z_i z_j^{-1}\). If it were, every cycle product would be identically the identity and the consistency loss would carry no information.
  • So \(U_{ij}\) is predicted independently by an edge encoder. Trivial node-derived edges are reported as a negative control.
gauge transformation and covariant residual \[ z_{i}\mapsto h_{i}z_{i},\qquad U_{ij}\mapsto h_{i}U_{ij}h_{j}^{-1} \] \[ r_{ij}=z_{i}^{-1}U_{ij}z_{j}\in G,\qquad C^{G}_{ij}=\exp\!\left[-\frac{d_{G}(r_{ij},1)^{2}}{\sigma_{c}^{2}}\right]\in(0,1] \]

THEOREM 3  Local gauge invariance of the covariant residual

A cyclic group is abelian, so \((h_i z_i)^{-1}(h_i U_{ij}h_j^{-1})(h_j z_j)=z_i^{-1}U_{ij}z_j\). The residual, and therefore the consistency score \(C^G_{ij}\), is independent of the local choice of basis — the same discipline of never using coordinate-dependent quantities as observables that the previous paper adopted.

Where the text is going

A target direction \(D_S\in G\) is obtained from the prompt, summary supervision, an evidence graph, or a pooling head. Alignment with the edge direction is normalized to \([-1,1]\), and since \(U_{ij}\) is oriented, in general \(A^{\mathrm{dir}}_{ij}\neq A^{\mathrm{dir}}_{ji}\).

direction alignment and group Fréchet medoid \[ A^{\mathrm{dir}}_{ij}=1-\frac{2\,d_{G}(U_{ij},D_{S})}{\lfloor q/2\rfloor}\in[-1,1] \] \[ D_{S}=\arg\min_{d\in G}\sum_{(i,j)\in E_{S}}\alpha_{ij}\,d_{G}(U_{ij},d)^{2} \]

The overall direction is a medoid on the group, not a mean angle. The group is finite, so it is found exactly by exhaustive search or histogram aggregation.

05 // HOLONOMY

Does the loop return to the identity?

Wilson loops and cycle risk

For a directed cycle \(C=(i_0,i_1,\dots,i_m=i_0)\), the holonomy is the product of the connections along it. Because the group is abelian, this is invariant under local gauge transformations.

holonomy and cycle risk \[ W_{C}=\prod_{t=0}^{m-1}U_{i_{t}i_{t+1}}\bmod p,\qquad R^{\mathrm{cyc}}_{C}=\left(\frac{2\,d_{G}(W_{C},1)}{q}\right)^{2} \]

A triangular cycle is the smallest possible test of whether three local inferences can hold at once.

THEOREM 4  Global integrability of a flat connection (the core result)

On a connected graph with \(U_{ji}=U_{ij}^{-1}\), if \(W_C=1\) for every cycle, then there exist vertex phases \(\{\zeta_i\in G\}\) with \(U_{ij}=\zeta_i\zeta_j^{-1}\) on every edge, and the family is unique up to a common left multiplication.

The proof fixes a root, defines \(\zeta_i\) as the product along any path to \(i\), and uses \(W_C=1\) to show path-independence. Read semantically: if local semantic transitions contradict each other on no cycle, they can be integrated into a single global assignment of semantic phases. This is the finite-cyclic-group counterpart of the connection, curvature and cohomological consistency of QuantumPhaseNet.

TRAINING — SOFT

Edge phase distributions \(\mathbf{u}_e\) are composed by cyclic convolution on the group, making \(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cyc}}\) differentiable in the real group algebra

INFERENCE — HARD

\(W_C\) is the modular-exponential product of hard group elements, and equality with the identity is an exact integer comparison

06 // ATTENTION

ModPhase Attention

Everything enters as a real-valued bias

Each phase head emits phase logits from the hidden state. Training uses a straight-through Gumbel-Softmax; inference takes the arg-max and looks the group element up in a precomputed table of \(\mathrm{pow}(g_h,a,p_h)\), so run time costs an \(O(1)\) integer lookup.

the ModPhase logit \[ \ell^{\mathrm{base}}_{ij}=\frac{\mathbf{q}_{i}^{\top}\mathbf{k}_{j}}{\sqrt{d_{k}}}+M_{ij} \] \[ \ell^{\mathrm{MP}}_{ij}=\ell^{\mathrm{base}}_{ij} +\alpha_{c}C^{G}_{ij}+\alpha_{h}H(i\to j)+\alpha_{d}A^{\mathrm{dir}}_{ij} -\alpha_{r}R^{\mathrm{ctx}}_{ij}+\alpha_{e}E^{\mathrm{evid}}_{ij} \]

\(R^{\mathrm{ctx}}_{ij}\) is the mean risk over short cycles containing the edge, and \(E^{\mathrm{evid}}_{ij}\in[0,1]\) is evidence support from retrieved documents, citations or a knowledge graph. Each \(\alpha_\bullet\ge0\) is parameterized through a softplus. Because every quantity computed in the phase group arrives as a real-valued scalar bias, no kernel of an existing Transformer implementation has to be modified — the main practical advantage of the design.

THEOREMS 5 & 6  Gauge invariance and boundedness

If the consistency, hierarchy, direction, cycle-risk and evidence scores are built only from gauge-invariant quantities, the whole logit is gauge invariant. And if \(\lVert v_j\rVert_2\le B_V\), the softmax weights are nonnegative and sum to one, so the output is a convex combination and \(\lVert Y_i\rVert_2\le B_V\).

⚠ WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
  • Hard quantization is discontinuous at cell boundaries, so no global Lipschitz continuity is claimed for the model as a whole.
  • A local Lipschitz estimate is available only for the relaxed model: soft distributions with \(\tau>0\), bounded logits, finite-distance kernels.
07 // RISK

Connection to hallucination risk

QuantumPhaseNet's decomposition into directional deviation, path deviation, curvature deviation, insufficient evidence and semantic uncertainty becomes concrete in the finite-group version below. The entropy \(\mathbb{H}(\mathbf{p}_t)\) is taken over semantic classes or the phase group, not over token strings.

risk score and calibration \[ \mathcal{R}^{\mathrm{MP}}_{t}=\beta_{h}(1-H_{t})+\beta_{d}(1-A^{\mathrm{dir}}_{t}) +\beta_{c}R^{\mathrm{cyc}}_{t}+\beta_{e}R^{\mathrm{evid}}_{t}+\beta_{u}\mathbb{H}(\mathbf{p}_{t}) \] \[ \widehat{P}(\mathrm{error}_{t}=1\mid\mathcal{R}_{t})=\sigma(a\mathcal{R}_{t}+b) \]

By definition \(\mathcal{R}^{\mathrm{MP}}_t\) is a score, not an error probability. A probabilistic reading is granted only after fitting a sigmoid or an isotonic regression on a calibration set, evaluated by Brier score, ECE, AUROC, AUPRC and risk–coverage curves. Above threshold, the system chooses among answering, re-retrieving, regenerating and abstaining. Any guarantee requires stating the assumptions of conformal abstention separately (Abbasi-Yadkori et al. 2024).

08 // TRAINING & INFERENCE

The two-layer structure

Modular exponentiation, arg-max and equality comparison are all non-differentiable. Leaving this vague would be a fatal weakness at review time, so the paper states the two-layer structure explicitly.

T1
SOFT PHASE
Phases are kept soft as \(\mathbf{p}_i(a)\) and depths as \(\mathbf{r}_i(\ell)\)
T2
STRAIGHT-THROUGH
Hard one-hot forward, soft probabilities backward — the default gradient estimator
T3
FOLDING & CONVOLUTION
Quotient projection via the folding matrix; cycle products via cyclic convolution of group distributions
T4
ANNEALING
The temperature \(\tau\) is annealed downward in stages while code usage is monitored
I1
HARD LOOKUP
Fix \(a=\arg\max\mathbf{p}(a)\) and look up \(z=\mathrm{pow}(g,a,p)\)
I2
INTEGER ARITHMETIC
Relative phases, quotient projections and cycle products are computed exactly in integers
I3
RISK GATE
Group kernels become real-valued biases; the calibrated risk selects answer / re-retrieve / regenerate / abstain

The NTT is an implementation device, not a semantics

When \(N\mid(p-1)\), an \(N\)-th primitive root allows the number theoretic transform over the finite field, computing the cyclic convolution of quantized group distributions in \(O(q\log q)\). But the paper is explicit that coefficients over \(\mathbb{F}_p\) are not given the energy interpretation of complex amplitudes: a finite field has no order or absolute value, so "a small index \(k\) is semantically low-frequency" does not follow automatically. Low-frequency semantics are established by supervision or by a spectral module on the real-valued side.

Complexity: it does not get faster

additional cost \[ T_{\mathrm{MP}}=O(NHd_{\phi})+O(NHL)+O(N^{2}H)+O(|\mathcal{C}|H) \]

Dense attention is \(O(N^2 d)\), and ModularPhaseNet does not improve that complexity. The claim is only that the pair-bias term \(O(N^2H)\) is of lower order when \(H\ll d\). The value lies not in acceleration but in the inductive bias of exact discrete composition, reproducibility, quotient projection and cycle checking. The recommended minimum setting is \(p=257,\ q=256,\ g=3\) (so \(q_\ell=2^\ell,\ \ell=0..8\)), starting from \(q=32\) or \(64\) to keep training cheap, with phase adapters inserted into the top two to four layers of a pretrained Transformer.

A worked example: \(p=17,\ q=16,\ g=3\)

With \(q_1=2,\ q_2=4,\ q_3=8,\ q_4=16\), the quotient projections act directly as a "concept address".

At \(q=2\) all four fall into the same animal class; at \(q=4\) the same mammal class; at \(q=8\) they split into \(\{\)dog, wolf\(\}\) and \(\{\)cat, lion\(\}\); at \(q=16\) they become individual concepts. Recording depth alongside the address expresses animal \(\preceq\) mammal \(\preceq\) canine \(\preceq\) dog as an asymmetric relation.

A consistent and an inconsistent triangle

consistent / inconsistent \[ U_{12}=3^{2}=9,\quad U_{23}=3^{3}=10,\quad U_{31}=3^{11}=7 \ \Longrightarrow\ W_{C}=9\cdot10\cdot7\equiv1 \pmod{17} \] \[ \text{mispredicting } U_{31}=3^{12}=4\ \Longrightarrow\ W_{C}=9\cdot10\cdot4\equiv3\not\equiv1 \pmod{17} \]

In the second case, three integer multiplications establish that no global phase assignment satisfies all three local inferences at once. That is the whole of the cycle-consistency test.

Falsification criteria

The paper declares in advance that reproducibly observing any of the following rejects or narrows the corresponding part of the central hypothesis.

FALSIFICATION CRITERIA
  • The quotient hierarchy fails to outperform a plain categorical embedding or an Order Embedding.
  • Performance varies substantially with a change of generator, so coordinate dependence cannot be controlled.
  • Cycle risk fails to correlate with contradiction labels and cannot be distinguished from a phase-shuffled control.
  • Increasing \(q\) fails to improve the approximation to the continuous version.
  • Brier score and ECE of the risk fail to beat an entropy baseline.
  • The improvement is explained entirely by more parameters or stronger retrieval.
  • Factuality gained per unit of extra compute is smaller than that of a verifier or reranker.
12 // LIMITS & REVIEWS

Limitations and anticipated reviewer comments

Q1
"The topology of a finite group is discrete and therefore trivial."
The novelty claim is restricted: it lies in the integration of the Cayley metric, the quotient filtration, the group-valued connection and cycle holonomy — not in the topology alone.
Q2
"\(C_q\cong\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}\), so modular exponentiation is unnecessary."
No increase in representational power is claimed. Modular exponentiation is an implementation representation giving exact composition, subgroup projection, NTT compatibility, checksums and reproducibility. A plain residue baseline is always included.
Q3
"Modular exponentiation is not differentiable."
Training-time group-algebra distributions with straight-through estimation are separated from hard inference-time operations, and the gradient estimator itself is ablated.
Q4
"If the cycle product is built from node differences it is always the identity."
Edge connections are predicted independently; trivial node-derived edges are reported as a negative control.
Q5
"A regular cyclic quotient cannot represent an irregular ontology."
Multiple heads, unused codes, depth masks and product codes are used, with comparison against Order and Poincaré baselines. No universal representability is claimed.

Limitations stated in the paper

STRUCTURAL

  • The modular-exponential representation is isomorphic to the exponent-residue one and creates no new capacity by itself
  • Small \(q\) causes aliasing at phase boundaries and code collisions
  • The word metric and the signed direction depend on the generator
  • A single chain of cyclic quotients assumes regular branching; multiple inheritance needs several heads or another geometry

PRACTICAL

  • Hard codes, temperature annealing and codebook collapse can destabilize training
  • A dense pair bias does not remove the quadratic complexity of attention
  • Group-code values carry no intrinsic natural-language meaning; they are identified only through supervision and invariants
  • Missing knowledge, retrieval failure and citation error are not explained by group consistency; evidence and calibration remain necessary
13 // CONCLUSION

Conclusion

ModularPhaseNet discretizes the continuous complex phase of QuantumPhaseNet into a finite cyclic group channel that coexists with a real-valued Transformer. Concept hierarchy is the nested partition of quotient groups together with depth, semantic direction is an oriented edge group element, and contextual consistency is cycle holonomy.

The theoretical value is that whether local semantic transitions integrate into a single global assignment becomes checkable on a finite group. The practical value is that hierarchy, direction, contradiction, evidence and uncertainty become observable within one and the same model, with no complex-valued model and no quantum hardware.

Reduction of hallucination is not a theorem but a calibratable empirical hypothesis whose inputs are exact invariants — and this paper fixes the plan and the falsification criteria before testing it.
