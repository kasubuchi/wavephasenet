Two papers by the same authors: an earlier one and its successor. The intuition carries over, but the mathematical stage was swapped out underneath it. What changed, what did not — and, just as importantly, what was left behind.

01 // TIMELINE

Six months

WavePhaseNet
Formalizes hallucination as the mismatch between a non-convex truth set and a convex \(L^p\) space. Builds a semantic conceptual hierarchy into the embedding space with the DFT and controls local contradiction through cohomology regularization. No experiments were run.
2026.07.23

Redefines phase as a quantity independent of the local frame, moving to a stage of manifolds, complex vector bundles and connections, and introducing quantum spectral selection and geometric risk. Still theory only at this point.
2026.07.30

Adds prototype experiments from the Validation Studio. RQ1–RQ4 were supported and RQ5 (quantum advantage) became a falsification candidate. The title now carries with Prototype Validation.

The move from one paper to the next is not a feature addition. It is the same intuition rebuilt on mathematics that breaks less easily. To see why that was worth doing, look at the weakness the earlier paper carried.

02 // SHARED

Five foundations that did not move

The stage changed; the roots did not.

How the problem is framed

Hallucination follows from the structure of the representation space, not from insufficient training. So rebuild the space, not the dataset. Both the diagnosis and the prescription carry over.

Frequency maps to meaning

Slowly varying components carry the intent of a whole document; rapidly varying ones carry local expression. This reading sits at the center of both papers.

Phase is never discarded

Both criticize FNet for dropping the imaginary part and thereby handling coreference and context dependency poorly. Hence "Phase" in both names.

The Transformer is not replaced

Both are designed as correction terms and residuals. Zero the coefficients and you recover the original model exactly, so it can be layered in and removed again.

Distrust of bolted-on knowledge

Thesauri and ontologies cannot be embedded directly, and RAG brings heavy external dependency with poor extensibility. So the hierarchy is built inside the space — a position both papers share.

04 // CORE

Two changes are doing the real work

The table has many rows, but two changes sit underneath. The rest follows from them.

① Phase: from coordinate to invariant

WAVEPHASENET

Two terms are compared through the difference of their phase angles. But \(\Phi_k\) is a coordinate — it depends on the chosen basis.

Writing \(\cos(\varphi_i - \varphi_j)\) silently assumes that every term shares one common reference axis.

→
QUANTUMPHASENET

Parallel transport is inserted before the inner product, so two points can be compared without fixing any reference axis.

That the value is unchanged under local gauge transformations is proved as a theorem.

covariant phase rate and coherence \[ \Omega_\gamma(t) = \operatorname{Im}\big\langle \hat\psi,\, D_t\hat\psi \big\rangle, \qquad C^{\nabla}_{ij} = \operatorname{Re}\big\langle \hat\psi_i,\ P^{\nabla}_{j\to i}\hat\psi_j \big\rangle \]

There is a price. Parallel transport depends on the path, so a convention must be fixed — the shortest geodesic, the path induced by textual order, or a learned reference path. That is one more implementation decision than the earlier paper required.

② From a line to an arbitrary graph

WAVEPHASENET

The DFT assumes tokens sit evenly spaced along a single line.

Relations that are not sequential — dependency, coreference, retrieved evidence — have no place in that framework.

→
QUANTUMPHASENET

Replacing it with low eigenmodes of a graph Laplacian performs the same low-frequency extraction over any connectivity structure.

The edge set can mix causal neighborhoods, k-nearest neighbors, syntactic dependency, coreference and retrieval-evidence edges.

This may matter more in practice. A document is not really a line of tokens; it is a network of references. The generalization also comes with the connection Laplacian, which measures smoothness while keeping each vector in its own tangent space.

05 // CONTINUITY

Two claims that did not carry over

The successor does not subsume the earlier paper. Two of its main claims simply do not appear.

① Cohomology and Hodge decomposition

The earlier paper's core — window coverings, cochains, coboundaries and semantic gluing through harmonic projection — has all but disappeared. Measuring local disagreement is now handled by curvature and geometric risk.

But the two measure different things. A coboundary measures disagreement between overlapping windows; curvature measures how far a semantic state fails to return to itself after traversing a closed contextual loop. Whether the latter subsumes the former is not obvious.

② The 3,000-dimension floor

Dimensionality reduction via cumulative energy analysis was called the "core contribution" of the earlier paper, yet the successor never mentions it. Its complexity discussion moves to sparse graphs, Lanczos and Chebyshev approximation instead.

Note also that the earlier figure (82–88% cumulative energy) is a derivation from the 1/f model, not a measurement. Since it can be checked by analyzing the embeddings of an existing model, it is the cheapest claim to test across either paper.

⚠ Reading both together

If the omissions are deliberate, nothing is wrong. But with both papers published side by side, a reader cannot easily tell whether the successor replaces the earlier work or complements it.

The accurate reading today is that the successor rebuilds part of the earlier paper on firmer mathematics — not all of it. For cohomology regularization and the dimensionality claim, the earlier paper remains the reference.
